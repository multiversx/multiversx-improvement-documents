The primary motivation for Supernova is to make MultiversX one of the fastest blockchains in existence without compromising security or decentralization

- Removes Execution Bottlenecks: Under the previous synchronous model, heavy transactions could consume up to 4.5 seconds of a 6-second round, preventing faster block times. By decoupling these processes, the network can process proposals in as little as 100ms
- Predictable Inclusion: A new Execution-Result Inclusion Estimator (EIE) ensures that nodes can deterministically decide how many execution results are safe to include, protecting minimum-spec hardware from falling behind
- Unchanged Economics: Despite the drastic increase in speed and round frequency, the end-of-epoch emission schedule and inflation remain identical to the current model
- Enhanced User Experience: With finality occurring in under a second, dApps and wallets will feel instantaneous to end-users
