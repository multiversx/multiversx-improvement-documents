Economics Changes \- Devnet
This proposal activates Phase 1 of the economics update: introduce a perpetual tail-inflation model with a burn, a builder-aligned fee market, and KPI-gated, demand-driven emissions. The aim is to ensure long-term security, expand usage and revenue, and tighten the feedback loop between network utility and EGLD value accrual
**Rationale**

* **Security budget forever:** Tail-inflation with burn eliminates dependence on volatile fee revenue alone.  
  **Builder incentives:** A large share of base fees goes to smart-contract authors to attract apps and talent.  
  **Reflexive value accrual:** Protocol activity → revenue → programmatic buybacks/burns → demand for EGLD.

**Specification (Phase 1 parameters — placeholders for UI)**

* **Annual tail-inflation cap:** `≤ X.XX%` (theoretical max; actual circulating inflation lower due to burns & KPI locks).  
  **Adaptive decay:** Quarterly adjustment using a composite KPI score (Staking, Burns/Revenue, DeFi TVL+Volumes; 3-month rolling average).  
  **Fee market:**  
  * Base fee split: **90% builders / 10% burn** in Year 1; shifts **–5pp/+5pp** per year toward **50/50** by Year 8\.  
  * Non-SC base fee: **100% burn**.  
  * Failed tx: **base \+ priority fully burned**.  
  * Priority fee: paid to validators (leader \+ consensus set).

* **Locked accelerator bucket:** Up to **YY%** of yearly emission locked unless KPI gates are met (example UI toggle).

**KPIs (illustrative UI fields)**

* **Staking ratio:** target band **65–70%** (APR adjusts via emissions; UI: slider \+ number).  
* **Revenue proxy:** growth in **on-chain burns** QoQ (UI: percent input).  
* **DeFi composite:** weighted function of **TVL** and **Volumes** (UI: weights \+ caps). [MultiversX Agora](https://agora.multiversx.com/t/a-competitive-economic-framework-for-multiversx-toward-revenue-and-reflexive-value-accrual/530)

**Risks & mitigations (for UI accordions)**

* **Over- or under-incentivization:** bounded by caps and quarterly decay rules.  
* **Volatility in activity:** rolling averages smooth noise; annual review adjusts targets.  
* **Builder capture risk:** fee split trends to **50/50** burn over 8 years to balance incentives
