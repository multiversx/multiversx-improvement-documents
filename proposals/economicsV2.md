# **The MultiversX Economic Evolution**

The current MultiversX economic model, based on a capped supply, was effective for establishing initial momentum for the network and distribution of the supply. However, to secure the network for the long term and ignite the next phase of significant growth, we must evolve. This proposal outlines a transition from a model of *passive to productive scarcity*, creating a self-reinforcing **Economic Flywheel** where network utility directly drives value back to EGLD.


## **1\. Why This Matters**

The network aims for a 10× expansion in liquidity, 10× network revenue and 10× token demand in the next cycle. Current float is high, usage moderate, and institutional connectivity limited. The new model is designed to transition from status-quo to growth-momentum. By aligning incentives for stakers, builders and institutional partners, the model seeks to lock supply, stimulate usage and create recurring demand.


## **2\. The Core Proposal: A Shift to a Growth-Oriented Economy**

We propose a comprehensive overhaul of the economic engine through four core pillars:

* **Pillar 1: A New Monetary Policy for Perpetual Security and Growth**  
  * **Action:** Replace the capped supply with a **decaying tail inflation model**, starting at a maximum theoretical rate of \~8.757% for the first year and adaptively decaying toward a 2-5% floor based on network growth KPIs.  
  * **Benefit:** This guarantees a **perpetual security budget** for validators and provides a sustainable engine to fund growth initiatives. 50% of these new emissions are programmatically locked unless clear growth targets are met, ensuring emissions are productive.  
  * **Translation:** competitive APR for staking, locks more supply, encourages long term commitment, attracts new liquidity.
 
* **Pillar 2: A Revitalized Fee Market to Reward Builders and Create Scarcity**  
  * **Action:** Implement a new fee market where **90% of transaction fees are allocated to builders**, and the remaining **10% are burned**. An optional "priority fee" goes entirely to validators.  
  * **Benefit:** This creates one of the most competitive developer incentives in the industry. The purpose is to **turn usage into revenue and token velocity**, while the burn mechanism creates **demand-driven deflation**.  
  * **Translation:** best in class system to rewards builders, catalyze on new revenue engine
 
* **Pillar 3: The Accelerator \- DAO-Governed Funds to Drive Activity**  
  * **Action:** A portion of emissions will be directed to two new DAO-governed funds to stimulate the ecosystem from both the supply (builder) and demand (user) sides.  
  * **Builder & Ecosystem Incentives:**  
    * **20% of emissions** will be allocated to builder credits to catalyze new revenue-generating projects.  
  * **New Staking & Growth Dividend Incentives:**  
    * Introduce an **APR of \~9.2% for simple staking**.  
    * **20% of emissions** will be allocated to the DeFi Growth Dividend resulting in an APR **of \~7.3% for “pro” staking** (users participating in active DeFi strategies).  
    * This creates a combined incentive pool of **\~16.5% per annum** aimed at attracting new liquidity, locking more supply, and encouraging long-term commitment.  
  * **Translation:** Improved staking yields make holding and staking EGLD more attractive.  
  * Builders receive stronger benefits to incentivize the creation and usage of new applications in the ecosystem.
 
* **Pillar 4: Reflexive Strategic Investments for Transformative Growth**  
  * **Action:** Introduce a new governance-gated mechanism, the **Reflexive Strategic Investment (RSI)** primitive, to fund high-impact growth opportunities.  
  * **Demand & Institutional Connectivity Catalysts:**  
    * **Digital Asset Treasury (DAT):** Establish a DAT as a bridge to stock market liquidity and recurring buy-back mechanisms, with a target of locking 20-30% of the token float.  
    * **Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF):** Develop an ETF vehicle to attract institutional capital and absorb EGLD supply from the open market.  
    * **U.S. Labs:** Create a U.S. entity for strategic growth, awareness, institutional deal-flow and major integrations.  
  * **Benefit & Safeguards:** These steps aim to create supply-locking and demand-side forces to reinforce token value and ecosystem scale. All RSI initiatives will require a **double opt-in governance mechanism**, which will be activated once the first deal is soft-committed.  
  * **Translation:** Institutional engagement increases demand, reduces float, and strengthens market dynamics—beneficial for all EGLD holders.


## **3\. Expected Outcomes**

This new framework is designed to achieve ambitious, concrete targets:

* **Capital Inflow:** Attract \>$100M in new capital and increase staked EGLD to over 65%.  
* **Revenue & Usage Growth:** Grow network revenue 100x (targeting $100k/day), expand to \>1,000 apps, and reach 1 million monthly active users.  
* **Value Accrual:** Create a virtuous cycle where network growth leads to increased fee burns and programmatic EGLD buybacks, perpetually reinforcing the value of the native asset.


## **4\. Implementation Plan**

* Upon approval, update staking smart contracts / protocol parameters to reflect new emissions.  
* Launch Accelerator DAO with clear terms & developer outreach.  
* Establish the institutional connectivity arms (DAT, ETF, U.S. Labs) in parallel.  
* Review at 12-month mark: compare actual KPIs vs target; adjust as necessary via future governance.


## **5\. The Governance Vote**

This proposal asks EGLD stakeholders to approve the adoption of this comprehensive new economic framework. Approval will authorize the phased implementation of the new emission and distribution model, the updated fee market, the DAO-governed Accelerator funds, as well as establish the RSI primitive, setting the foundation for the next era of growth for MultiversX.
