We want to attract more liquidity to the network. But what does liquidity want?

Liquidity seeks one thing above all: the prospect of growth.

To reignite positive momentum, we have defined a new, competitive incentive structure:  
 (a) 9.2% APR for simple staking, and  
 (b) average 7.3% APR for pro staking

Together, these buckets form a combined reward of up to 16.5% per annum.

This is a precise and deliberate signal for new liquidity to enter, to stay, and multiply. A first foundational pillar pairing stirring competitive incentives, with a quest to attract 10x liquidity.

New liquidity means new buy demand. Furthermore, new liquidity takes float supply out of circulation, and locks it in as productive capital.

The snowball has just begun to move, once again. For new liquidity, this is the time to deploy.

## **3\. Competitive Builder Incentives, Jump-starting the Revenue Machine**

We want more network revenue, but how do we engineer toward that?

Well, revenue comes through usage. Usage comes through users. The more applications are built on top of the network, the more potential for users, the more potential for usage.

Thus, we can significantly increase the revenue, by dramatically growing the number of users, and expanding their usage behavior.

But how do we do that?

Two incentives meant to accelerate builder cycles:

(a) 90% of transactions fees go to builders directly  
 (b) 20% jump-starts new revenue engines via builder credits

Together, these two steps set a robust incentive package in place, one a clear quest to 10x network revenue.

But why does revenue matter?

More network usage means greater EGLD velocity within the system. It means more capital productivity, and less capital leaving it. It means less supply float, tighter inelasticity, and stronger buy side impact.
